About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery started in 2012 with one small oven and a family sourdough starter. What began as weekend bread sales for our neighbors grew into a corner bakery where locals gather every morning. Our goal has always been simple: bake honest food by hand and share it with our community.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We buy flour from regional mills, eggs and dairy from nearby farms, and fruit that is in season. Working with local growers means fresher ingredients, better flavor, and a stronger neighborhood economy.
